## Overview
Developed automated **control analytics and risk monitoring systems** for senior management across global regions (APAC, EMEA, Americas), enabling real-time oversight of operational controls and regulatory compliance.

**Key Contributions:**
- Built automated reporting pipelines processing millions of daily transactions for control effectiveness monitoring
- Developed anomaly detection systems flagging control breaches and operational risks in real-time
- Created executive dashboards with predictive analytics for proactive risk management
- Implemented data quality frameworks ensuring accuracy of regulatory reporting to senior leadership

**Technical Approach:**
- ETL pipelines aggregating data from 20+ source systems across global operations
- Python-based analytics engine with statistical modeling for control effectiveness scoring
- Interactive dashboards (Tableau/PowerBI) providing drill-down capabilities from global to transaction level
- Automated alert systems for critical control violations requiring immediate action

**Impact:** Reports directly informed decision-making for VPs and C-level executives managing operational risk across $2T+ in assets under custody.

**Tech Stack:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), SQL (Teradata, SQL Server), Tableau, PowerBI, Airflow, Git
