## Organization
Google Developer Student Club (GDSC), Northeastern University, Boston

## Duration
Aug 2023 – July 2024

## Overview
Founded and led the university's GDSC chapter during a one-year term, building a technical community within Northeastern University. Drove the student-club recognition process with the university, formed the core team, and selected a faculty advisor to support the organization. Hosted workshops and events — covering Git, Linux, GitHub, and Google Cloud — alongside speaker sessions and fireside chats, growing student knowledge of developer products and platforms through hands-on learning.
