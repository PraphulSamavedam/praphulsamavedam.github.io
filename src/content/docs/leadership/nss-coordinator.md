## Organization
NSS (National Service Scheme), BITS-Pilani

## Duration
June 2016 – May 2017

## Overview
Single point of contact for the schooling department. Managed 74 volunteers, revamped department structure from "team heads" to a more efficient "class heads" system. Introduced the Nav Gurukul program — two underprivileged students were selected for free education at a top institute through 10+2. Led enrollment of 6 kids of construction workers in partnership with Nirmaan NGO.
