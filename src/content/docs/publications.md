## IEEE Conference Paper

**Energy Harvesting, Hybrid, Cooperative Wireless System Modeling and Outage Performance Analysis**

*Samavedam M. Praphul, Bindu Priya Kalathuru, Sainath Bitragunta*

Published in **2018 Conference on Information and Communication Technology (CICT)**, IEEE, 2019.

This paper presents a hybrid cooperative wireless system consisting of analog and digital links with energy harvesting relay nodes. Relay nodes harvest energy for a small τ fraction and use this harvested energy to process and relay the input signal. The paper derives closed-form outage probability expressions and demonstrates the effectiveness of Pre-Emphasis (PE) and De-Emphasis (DE) signal enhancement techniques for lowering outage probability.
